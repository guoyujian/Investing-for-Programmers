# Sync Targets

## `问答笔记.md`

Use for:

- direct user questions and concise durable answers
- clarifications that are likely to recur

Do not use for:

- large chapter summaries
- progress-only changes

## Chapter Notes

Use for:

- deeper concept explanations
- example walkthroughs
- figure or listing interpretation
- end-of-chapter restructuring

## `notes/GLOSSARY.md`

Use for:

- terms that will likely appear again
- Chinese plus English pairs where translation can drift

## `学习进度.md`

Use for:

- chapter started
- first-round handout completed
- second-round deep read pending or completed
- skip decisions

## `全书总笔记.md`

Use for:

- book-level conclusions
- cross-chapter patterns
- stage wrap-up and honest completion status
